\chapter{The Return}

% % Replace with a short epigraph and its attribution.
\epigraph{Coming home is a way to begin again.}{An old song}

% % Replace with the opening scene: the journey back.
The return begins with a light suitcase and a ticket in a pocket. Fields and
low houses pass by the window, marking the minutes. No one expects anything
grand: just to arrive, say hello and sit at the table. The simple gesture
holds the line that keeps the house together, \enquote{good things take time}.

% % Replace with the development: what is waiting at home.
When the traveller arrives, everything is in its place, though something
has shifted. The kitchen smells of bread, the yard holds the same chair and
the clock shows its usual hour. News is shared slowly and out of order as
evening settles over the rooftops.

% % Replace with the closing: what remains after the return.
At night there is no speech or promise, only a clear agreement: care for
what is near and begin again each day. The book closes here, with the house
quiet and a lamp left on for whoever arrives next.
